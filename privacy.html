Privacy Policy / Kebijakan Privasi

Last updated / Terakhir diperbarui: 25 November 2026

English

PinaraK is a private internal tool used by the social media team of Nadheera (Arabelle) to read analytics of its own social media accounts and to help reply to customer messages. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

With the account owner's explicit authorization through TikTok Login Kit, PinaraK reads only the account's basic profile (display name, avatar) and its list of public videos (title, cover, share link and public counts such as views, likes, comments and shares).

How we use it

The data is shown only to the account owner's team inside PinaraK to make performance reports. We do not sell, share or publish this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored in the team's private Google Sheet. Access tokens are stored privately and never shown. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps), and can ask us to delete all stored data by emailing the contact below.

Bahasa Indonesia

PinaraK adalah alat internal pribadi tim sosial media Nadheera (Arabelle) untuk membaca data performa akun sendiri dan membantu membalas pesan pelanggan. Tidak ditawarkan ke publik.

Atas izin pemilik akun lewat TikTok Login Kit, PinaraK hanya membaca profil dasar dan daftar video publik (judul, cover, tautan, serta jumlah tayangan, suka, komentar, bagikan). Data hanya dilihat tim pemilik akun untuk laporan performa, tidak dijual, dibagikan, atau dipakai untuk iklan. Data disimpan di Google Sheet pribadi tim. Izin bisa dicabut kapan saja di pengaturan TikTok, dan data bisa dihapus dengan menghubungi kontak di bawah.
